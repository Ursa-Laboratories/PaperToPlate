# Source papers and assets

The three source papers in `research/three-assay-review/sources/` and their
copies in `review-pack/sources/` retain their original title pages and license
notices. Each supplied ChemRxiv version states **CC BY 4.0**. The review, JSON
extractions and page images identify their sources; calculations and unresolved
details are distinguished from reported procedures. Original PDFs are unchanged.

- Anna M. Dorfmüller, Mark M. Pradja, Christian Modrzynski and Jonathan Z. Bloh,
  *Rapid and Facile Spectrophotometric Method for the Selective Determination of
  Peroxymonosulfate, Peroxydisulfate and Hydrogen Peroxide in Ternary Mixtures*,
  [ChemRxiv version 1](https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv.15000337/v1).
- Kylie Yang, Lana C. Lee, Hiral A. Kotak, Evelyn R. Morton, Soo Mei Chee,
  Duy P. M. Nguyen, Alvaro Keskküla and Cally J. E. Haynes,
  *High-throughput and Automated Anion Transport Assays*,
  [ChemRxiv](https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv-2024-kxf0x).
- Katja F. Hellendahl, Maryke Fehlau, Sebastian Hans, Peter Neubauer and
  Anke Kurreck, *Semi-automated high-throughput substrate screening assay for
  nucleoside kinases*, [ChemRxiv](https://doi.org/10.26434/chemrxiv-2021-k0w7q).
